Was working through a problem the other day. I asked claude to help design a solution.

There was a problem with the design it gave me, so I started trying to solve that. I got stuck.

Eventually, I asked a co-worker. "What do you think of this?"

After some back and forth, he asked "What's the original problem you were trying to solve?"

I realized I had over-indexed on solving the problem with what I though could be a solution to the point that I never properly evaluated the solution against the original problem in the first place.

And it turned out a much better solution was not only much different, but it was much simpler.
