The JN sticky note in ../favicon.svg is drawn from the J and N glyphs of Josh
Noll Hand (../fonts/josh-noll-hand/josh-noll-hand.woff2), converted to paths so
the icon doesn't load the font. The glyph outlines were flattened, simplified to
about 0.1 icon units, and stroked 2.4 units heavier so they read at small sizes.

PNGs were rendered from that SVG with resvg. favicon-16x16.png uses a 3.2-unit
stroke for legibility at 16px. Both ICO files package the 16px and 32px PNGs.
